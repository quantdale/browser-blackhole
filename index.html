This canvas renders a live, per-pixel ray-traced view of the selected destination. In Cosmic Atlas the view is computed by tracing camera rays through curved spacetime, so the black-hole shadow, accretion-disk lensing and frequency shifts follow from the geometry. On the legacy diagnostic route (?legacy=1) the same canvas shows a deterministic test gradient instead: red increases to the right, green increases toward the top, and blue encodes the depth axis of the camera ray direction.
