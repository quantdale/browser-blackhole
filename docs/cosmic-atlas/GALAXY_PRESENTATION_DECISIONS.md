# Galaxy Collision presentation decisions

## Optional dust/gas layer — deferred

GC1 supplies authoritative tracer positions and does not ship a validated gas
field. A procedural dust/gas layer was prototyped conceptually, but the
current cinematic captures already have a clear bridge/tail and nucleus
hierarchy from the GC1 backbone plus bounded unresolved stellar emitters.
Adding an unanchored opaque dust layer would risk implying source-data or gas
fidelity that the destination cannot claim and would reduce the sparse
structure that makes the bridge readable. It is deferred until a disclosed
gas proxy and a visual/performance comparison justify it.

## Star-forming knots — rejected for the current contract

No star-formation-rate or gas-density state exists in GC1. Deterministic knots
would therefore be arbitrary decorations rather than source-derived emitters.
The bounded unresolved stellar population is sufficient for the current
cinematic goal and is explicitly labeled as procedural offsets around GC1
tracers. Star-forming knots remain rejected unless a future data-backed or
explicitly authored proxy is added with its own disclosure and review gate.
